## ITB Red-Team Empirical Validation

> **Security notice.** ITB is an experimental symmetric cipher construction without prior peer review, independent cryptanalysis, or formal certification. The construction's security properties have **not been verified** by independent cryptographers or mathematicians.
>
> PRF-grade hash functions are **required**. No warranty is provided.

**No bespoke cryptography.** ITB composes established, standardized primitives rather than introducing new cryptographic designs. Security properties and regulatory status are inherited from the underlying primitives; see [README.md](README.md) for jurisdictional certification details.

This document records the adversarial validation of the shipped ITB construction. The construction under test is **Triple Ouroboros** — the byte-level 3-region split — over the **8-seed** constellation, with the **Interlocked Barrier** as its core.

**[FAQ.md](FAQ.md)** — plain-language analytical and empirical walkthrough of deliberately broken primitives under the shipped barrier: 8-bit total PRF failure and inversion (`nullHash`, `quadHash`, `trainHash`) alongside full-width algebraic stress controls (`jokeHash`, GF(2)-linear `CRC128`, T-function `FNV-1a`).

## The Interlocked Barrier is a two-part composition

Every verdict below is read against the **full** barrier, which is two layers acting in series, not one. Treating either Barrier in isolation misstates what the construction resists.

**Rank Barrier — the 48-bit interlock permutation** (`interlock48.go`). Before COBS framing and pixel encoding, the interleaved payload is chunked into 48-bit (6-byte) words (or 168-bit / 21-byte words in `168-bit` mode). Each chunk is partitioned into three disjoint 16-of-48 lanes by a balanced mask triple `(m0, m1, m2)` — `popcount(mᵢ) = 16`, `m0 ∪ m1 ∪ m2 = 2^48 − 1` — drawn per chunk from a space of ≈ 2^70.20 balanced partitions, PRF-keyed by the `lockSeed` and the nonce (`rankToMaskTriple48`, `chunk48lock`, `splitForTriple48LockedInto`). Under the **Desynchronized Interlocked Barrier** (**Layer II**; [SCIENCE.md § 1.2.1](SCIENCE.md#121-desynchronized-interlocked-barrier), [PROOFS.md Proof 11](PROOFS.md#proof-11-48-bit-rank-barrier-mask-space-interlocked-barrier)), domain tag `0x06` (folded via `fullWidthFold`) drives cross-lane slot shifts Σ_ℓ (`laneSlotShift`, `desyncBeta0`, `desyncBeta1` with β_0 ≠ β_1) across the M chunk slots of lanes 0 and 1 before COBS framing, so chunk j's lane fragments land at non-co-located slot indices with zero synchronous slot pairs μ_0(j) ≠ μ_1(j) (`desync_beta0_ne_beta1`, `desync_no_fixed_slot_pair` in `lean/ITB/Desync.lean`). The mapping from a plaintext bit to the lane and chunk slot it lands in is a hidden per-chunk secret. Rank Barrier alone is a keyed permutation: it re-orders bits but does not XOR key material into them.

**Pixel Barrier — the per-pixel absorption stage** (`process_pixels.c`). Each pixel packs 56 payload bits into 8 channel bytes of 7 data bits each. Per pixel, keyed by the `dataSeed` / `noiseSeed` ChainHash outputs, the encode path (Phase 3 XOR, Phase 4 rotate, Phase 5 noise-insert; scalar tail at the same phase structure) applies, per 7-bit field: a **channelXOR** with 7 PRF-mask bits, a **rotate7** by a per-pixel amount in `[0,6]`, and insertion around a **per-pixel noise bit** taken from the original DRBG container byte (filled by `internal/drbg` — CSPRNG-seeded noise from the operator-selected DRBG fill primitive, AES-256-CTR by default where hardware AES is present and ChaCha20 otherwise) at a per-pixel `noisePos`. The decode path inverts exactly (noise-strip, inverse rotate, XOR). `rotateBits7` and the noise merge are the core of [Proof 1](PROOFS.md#proof-1-information-theoretic-barrier) / [Proof 4](PROOFS.md#proof-4-rotation-barrier) / [Proof 5](PROOFS.md#proof-5-noise-barrier-bound) / [Proof 7](PROOFS.md#proof-7-bias-neutralization). Pixel Barrier alone is an XOR-with-key-material-plus-noise stage: it masks each byte to the 1:1 signal/noise floor but does not permute bit-to-position.

**Together** the two Barriers close the COA / KPA / CPA families under the PRF assumption. Rank Barrier denies the attacker a stable bit-position-to-lane anchor; Pixel Barrier denies a per-byte observation channel (`P(observed | hash) = 1/2`). A crib that is known plaintext still reaches neither a fixed position (Rank Barrier) nor an observable byte (Pixel Barrier). Neither Barrier alone is sufficient — Rank Barrier alone is invertible once a primitive is inverted, Pixel Barrier alone leaves a fixed bit-position map — and the shipped construction never runs either alone.

**8-seed isolation** (`seed256.go` / `seed512.go`). 8 seeds are drawn as independent CSPRNG components and enforced pairwise-distinct at the API by byte-level `Components` comparison in addition to pointer identity (so byte-identical material reaching the API through blob import or the Low-Level constructors is rejected on the same gate): `noiseSeed`, `lockSeed`, `dataSeed1..3`, `startSeed1..3`. No seed is reused across the three regions. The construction employs dual-nonce placement: the public main nonce `N_m` resides in cleartext in the chunk header on the wire `[prefix(32)][main_nonce][W][H][container]`, while a dedicated per-message interlock nonce `N_il` is split across the three container lane buffers ahead of COBS framing under the Pixel Barrier. For each message, `N_il` is absorbed through the full ChainHash cascade by `deriveInterLockSeed` (`seed128.go` / `seed256.go` / `seed512.go`): the dedicated interlock domain tag `0x04` plus `N_il` feed the chained hash, keying the Two-Stage Cascade Fill primer round on intermediate key `K` (which then evaluates per-chunk rank fill under tag `0x03` and cross-lane slot desynchronization Σ_ℓ under tag `0x06` via `fullWidthFold`); all six domain tags `0x01..0x06` (`0x01` for MAC/KDF, `0x02` for `deriveStartPixel`, `0x05` for the **Layer I** sub-region schedule Π_r via `fullWidthFold`) are pairwise distinct (`desync_domain_tags_pairwise_distinct` in `lean/ITB/Desync.lean`), so the derivations stay decorrelated even for byte-identical seed material. Full knowledge of any seed subset yields zero bits on the rest; the `lockSeed` that keys the Rank Barrier is isolated from the `noiseSeed` that keys the noise channel and from the per-region `dataSeed`/`startSeed`.

**Three-region construction.** Each of the three regions has its own `startPixel`, derived from its own `startSeed` and the nonce (`0x02` tag), and **different per region**. Under the **Desynchronized Interlocked Barrier** (**Layer I**; [SCIENCE.md § 1.2.1](SCIENCE.md#121-desynchronized-interlocked-barrier), [PROOFS.md Proof 4a](PROOFS.md#proof-4a-multi-factor-full-kpa-resistance)), each region r is partitioned into K_r secret-bounded sub-regions (`K_r = 6 + (w_0 mod 5) ∈ [6, 10]` for `P_r < 365`; `K_r = 8 + (w_0 mod 9) ∈ [8, 16]` for `P_r ≥ 365`) with telescopic boundary jitter and local cyclic offsets (`subregionAddr`, `effectiveStartPixel`) traversed in a secret permutation Π_r derived from `startSeed_r` and `N_m` under domain tag `0x05` via `fullWidthFold`. None of the three regional schedules or entry offsets is transmitted. An attacker holding the container does not know where any region's payload begins or where its sub-region boundaries lie, so no plaintext boundary is visible in any region; there is no start marker and no end marker on the wire.

## Construction under test and shared conventions

Every track drives the shipped core Triple entrypoints (`Encrypt3x256Cfg` / `Encrypt3x512Cfg` and their decrypt duals, or the streaming `EncryptStream3xCfg` / `EncryptStreamAuth3xCfg` families) over the 8 mandatory distinct seeds. Parallax and the outer cipher wrapper are independent layers above the barrier; except where a probe explicitly toggles the wrapper, the measured surface is the barrier layer alone.

**Discipline carried on every claim.** Every empirical verdict is **sample-bounded**: a null result means no measurable signal above the finite-sample floor at the stated sample size N under the stated threat model, never that no signal exists. PRF-grade closure claims are **PRF-conditional**. The independent-stream reference throughout is the byte-equal rate of two uniform random streams, **1/256 ≈ 0.00391**.

**Nonce reuse is a lab-only assumption.** A caller cannot force nonce reuse — the nonce is drawn from `crypto/rand` on every call — but it is the only condition under which the empirical record has ever measured signal, so several probes below adopt it to give the attacker its best case.

**Attacker-realism.** Every recovery decision uses only attacker-visible inputs (ciphertext bytes, the public crib, the public nonce and dimension header, the seed-independent public per-pixel `const`); ground-truth seed values appear only in terminal-stage audit printouts, never in a decision.

**KL matrix — end-to-end indistinguishability from `/dev/urandom`.** A construction-level Mode B χ² / pairwise-KL distinguisher measures, for the shipping wire under every combination of plaintext size and Barrier Fill margin, whether the ITB body bytes are separable from `/dev/urandom` bytes of matched length. Fixed BLAKE3 keys the ChainHash (a single representative PRF-grade primitive suffices per the closure argument in [PRF-grade closure](#prf-grade-closure-vs-broken-primitive-under-determination)). Each cell of the 11 sizes × 6 Barrier Fill values grid draws 25 ITB samples plus 25 `/dev/urandom` samples at matched body size, computes the mean discriminator ratio on each side, and z-scores the difference by the pooled standard deviation.

| Size ↓ / BF → | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 16 | 32 |
|:---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1 KB / 4 KB / 8 KB / 32 KB / 64 KB / 128 KB / 256 KB / 512 KB / 1 MB / 2 MB / 4 MB | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

All 66 cells satisfy `|z_ratio| ≤ 1.0` (indistinguishable at 1 σ). Peak `z_ratio` across the grid is **0.49** (64 KB, BF = 1) and peak `z(χ²)` is **0.48** (512 KB, BF = 8); every other cell sits below those bounds. Minimum Barrier Fill margin for the indistinguishability verdict is **BF = 1 for every size measured** — the shipped default `DefaultBarrierFill = 1` is already sufficient across the 1 KB → 4 MB payload envelope, and increasing BF up to 32 produces no measurable improvement in the discriminator.

The empirical result matches the architectural expectation above: the mask-space cardinality per chunk (≈ 2^70.20) and the per-chunk bias (≈ 2^-57.8) are architectural guarantees; the KL matrix is the deployment-shape observation that a Mode B distinguisher against the attacker-visible wire cannot separate ITB from `/dev/urandom` bytes at any size × BF in the shipping envelope. ## Per-attack verdicts

Each verdict assumes the full barrier active (shipped default, non-disableable) and, where it invokes primitive strength, the PRF assumption and fresh per-message nonces. BLAKE3 is the representative PRF-grade primitive; the closure argument consumes only the PRF property, not any BLAKE3-specific structure, so a single representative generalises across the PRF-grade registry subset (see [PRF-grade closure](#prf-grade-closure-vs-broken-primitive-under-determination)). Empirically corroborated on the BLAKE3 representative by the KL matrix above at the tested sample sizes.

Every "closed under PRF" verdict above is **instance-formulation-bounded and sample-bounded** per the per-attack sections that follow; total PRF inversion falls outside the barrier's protection ([Proof 4a Asymmetry note](PROOFS.md#proof-4a-multi-factor-full-kpa-resistance)).

### COA — ciphertext-only

Under passive observation the wire is `[prefix(32)][main_nonce][W][H][container]` per chunk record (one record for a Single Message, one per chunk for a stream), the prefix being CSPRNG output. The container bytes are individually uniform: every 48-bit chunk is bit-permuted across lanes (Rank Barrier), COBS-framed per lane, and each 7-bit channel field is XOR-masked and rotated with a fresh DRBG noise bit inserted at a secret position (Pixel Barrier). Measured container-body byte-equal and zero rates sit at the 1/256 floor at the tested sample sizes; the only non-uniform bytes on the wire are the fixed cleartext dimension header (§4.0), which restates the container size the wire length already exposes. No plaintext, key, or boundary channel is present.

### Crib KPA and Full KPA

**Threat model.** Crib / Full KPA under the PRF assumption with fresh nonces; the Nonce reuse variant is treated separately. **Verdict — closed at the instance-formulation layer, sample-bounded.** The 4-probe minimum drives BLAKE3 through the 256-bit core Triple entrypoint (`redteam_prf_blake3_test.go`, `go test -tags redteam -run TestRedteamPRF -v -count=1 ./`, combined wall-clock under one second):

| Probe | Threat model | Sample | Observable | Floor | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Crib KPA, fresh nonce | Crib KPA, PRF, fresh nonce | N = 200, 4 KB, shared crib | container-head byte-equal **0.00430**; \|Pearson\| **0.01039** | 0.00391 | **null** — crib anchors nothing |
| 2 — Nonce reuse | Nonce reuse, **lab-only** (dual-slot collision) | N = 200, both nonces forced identical | full-wire byte-equal **0.02559**; \|Pearson\| **0.02236** | 0.00391 | the 68 identical chunk-header bytes contribute ≈ 0.0123 (≈ 48 %) of the rate; the remainder sits in the container body, where the shared crib and the collided interlock-nonce fragments repeat under Scenario A — see [Nonce reuse](#nonce-reuse-lab-only) |
| 3 — Related-seed Δ | Related-seed, **lab-only** | N = 128, 1-bit Δ, fixed nonce | dataSeed1 Δ bit-diff **0.23805**; lockSeed Δ **0.48813** | diffused | **null for exploitability** |
| 4 — Full KPA, shared state | Full KPA, PRF, fresh nonce | N = 64 pairs, shared bundle | pairwise byte-equal **0.00464**; all round-trips exact | 0.00391 | **null** — no aggregable correlation |

The architectural reason is the two-part composition. Rank Barrier: each 48-bit chunk draws its mask triple from ≈ 2^70.20 balanced partitions, so a crib fixes no bit-position-to-lane mapping a solver could anchor; even granting the 48 known bits of a chunk under Full KPA, the mask has ≈ 2^57.80 preimages and is unobservable without the `lockSeed`, so no per-bit constraint can be written down. Pixel Barrier: the per-pixel 1:1 signal/noise ambiguity ([Proof 1](PROOFS.md#proof-1-information-theoretic-barrier)) leaves no observed byte that ranks candidates. Seed recovery is **instance-formulation-bounded, not solver-bounded** — the seed-recovery brute force the architecture rules out is documented, not run against wall-clock. The residual honest caveat: **systematic partial PRF inversion is raised in cost but not eliminated, and total PRF inversion circumvents the barrier entirely.**

### Partial KPA

No dedicated probe is run: the result follows a fortiori from the Full / Crib KPA null. A Partial KPA attacker holds strictly less known plaintext, so its achievable-key sets per pixel are a superset of the full-crib sets and its cross-pixel intersection can only be larger — it recovers no key where the stronger full-crib attacker recovers none. The `gcd(7,8)=1` byte-splitting obstacle (every plaintext byte spans two channels; a missing adjacent byte blocks per-channel candidate formulation) is an additional independent factor active only under Partial KPA.

### CPA — chosen-plaintext, fresh nonce

Under fresh-nonce CPA the attack surface reduces to statistical ciphertext properties. Each message draws fresh masks and **Layer II** cross-lane slot shifts (Rank Barrier), fresh noise positions, rotations, startPixels, and **Layer I** sub-region schedules (Pixel Barrier); chosen plaintext gives no observable handle on any of these because the lane assignment is a hidden per-chunk secret and each byte is at the 1:1 floor. The KL matrix above places the shipped wire at the finite-sample statistical floor — indistinguishable from `/dev/urandom` within the tested tolerance — on the BLAKE3 representative at every size × Barrier Fill cell measured, and the [fresh-nonce CPA probe](#fresh-nonce-cpa-under-fnv-1a-on-every-seed-role) holds the container body at the df = 255 uniform band and the pair byte-equal rate at the 1/256 floor on both its FNV-1a and BLAKE3 arms across every chosen-plaintext kind tested. The verdict is **closure under the PRF assumption with fresh nonces**, sample-bounded (scoped to the wire container body and payload-channel probes; see [§4.0](#40-layer-attribution--what-actually-appears-on-the-wire)).

### Nonce reuse (lab-only)

Nonce reuse is **not reachable through the shipped API** — every message draws two independent CSPRNG nonces (`main_nonce` and `interlock_nonce`) and `generateNoncePairCfg` re-draws whenever both are on the CSPRNG path. Forced via the two test-only overrides (`testNonceOverride` / `testInterlockNonceOverride`), three collision classes surface distinctly, each mapping to which of the two independent nonce-driven axes the attacker holds fixed across the compared pair:

- **Scenario A — dual-slot collision.** Both nonces collide across the pair, with `main_nonce ≠ interlock_nonce` within each message (the shipped `generateNoncePairCfg` distinctness invariant, held under lab override). Every nonce-bound derivation slot repeats: per-pixel `noisePos` and per-region rotation / `channelXOR` / `startPixel` (`0x02`) / **Layer I** sub-region trajectory (`0x05`) from the main-nonce path, and the per-chunk 48-bit interlock mask triple (`0x03`) / **Layer II** cross-lane slot shifts (`0x06`) from the interlock-nonce path (`0x04`). Reproduces a full nonce-reuse event across both nonces — the maximum leverage a lab attacker can force.
- **Scenario B — main-only collision.** `main_nonce` is fixed across the pair; `interlock_nonce` is drawn from `crypto/rand` per encrypt. Reproduces a buggy caller who reuses the seven `main_nonce`-keyed derivation slots (`noisePos`, `dataSeed_i`, `startPixel_i` / **Layer I** `Π_r`) but leaves the eighth (`lockSeed`-keyed per-chunk mask draw `0x03` and **Layer II** slot shifts `0x06`) PRF-parameterised. The interlock permutation differs across the pair.
- **Scenario C — interlock-only collision.** Mirror of B — `interlock_nonce` is fixed across the pair; `main_nonce` is drawn per encrypt. The interlock mask triple and **Layer II** slot shifts repeat; the seven main-nonce-keyed derivation slots re-parametrise independently.

Under all three scenarios the fresh DRBG noise bit that the Pixel Barrier inserts at each pixel's `noisePos` and the fresh DRBG tail-fill beyond the COBS terminator are drawn per-encrypt and do not repeat. Under Scenario A, `C1 XOR C2` on the container body carries `rotate7(region_XOR_bits, r)` at every non-noise bit position and a fresh random bit at the noise position — the classical two-time-pad structure a stream cipher would present, present **only up to the Rank Barrier's permutation of the plaintext bits into three lane-scrambled region payloads**. Under Scenarios B and C, one of the two independent axes is PRF-re-parametrised across the pair, so even that lane-scrambled two-time-pad structure disappears from the container body statistics.

**Empirical measurement** — `redteam_nonce_reuse_dualnonce_test.go`, dual primitive spectrum: **BLAKE3** PRF reference and **FNV-1a** below-spec stress on every one of the 8 seed roles (independent random component vectors per seed; the probe drives both primitives through the 128-bit `HashFunc128` interface). All measurements are attacker-visible — every statistic reads container-body bytes and the public wire header only. The deterministic wire header bytes are stripped from every statistic so under Scenario A the collided-header bytes do not swamp the barrier signal by contributing perfectly correlated bytes.

Three axis-attributions the matrix isolates:

- **Random-plaintext container-body statistics track the 1/256 floor under Scenarios B and C.** Byte-equal ratio stays in `[0.97×, 1.07×]`, |Pearson| below 0.012, Hamming bit-diff within 0.001 of the ideal 0.5, and χ² stays inside the one-sided 3σ df=255 uniform band (`219.1 – 308.9` across all 24 Scenario B and C cells). Under Scenario A the random rows sit at 2.95× / 2.77× / 1.46× the floor at 512 B / 4 KB / 16 KB (chi² 4 157 – 17 788 across both primitives): a constant ≈ 39 excess equal bytes per pair at every size, from the interlock-nonce fragments (64 bytes at `NonceBits` 512) that ride inside the container and repeat at the same pixels under the dual-slot collision, each deterministic byte matching at ≈ 1/2 behind its fresh noise bit — a fixed-size region diluted by container size, not a plaintext-recovery channel.
- **Near-identical-pair residue is strictly a Scenario-A-only signature; both Scenarios B and C dissolve it to the 1/256 floor.** Under Scenario A at 4 KB, `bodyByte-equal ≈ 0.443` — every deterministic byte carries seven identical data bits with one fresh DRBG noise bit, so ~1/2 of body bytes are equal by construction and the plaintext-difference contribution is confined to the ~0.15 % of chunks around the single-bit flip. Under Scenario B, the fresh `interlock_nonce` redraws both the per-chunk 48-bit mask triple (`0x03`) and **Layer II**'s cross-lane slot shifts Σ_ℓ (`0x06`, `laneSlotShift` across `M^3` chunk-slot triples), dispersing identical plaintext chunks across different lane slot indices per message and dropping the near-identical residue to floor-level (`1.00×–1.05×` floor, χ² `219.1–308.9`, vs `~1.5×` floor and χ² `10.7k–12.9k` when slot shifts are disabled via `ITB_BARRIERS_DESYNC=off`). Under Scenario C the entire Pixel Barrier per-pixel pipeline redraws per encrypt (channelXOR, rotation, noisePos, startPixel `0x02`, and **Layer I** sub-region schedule `0x05`), likewise dropping the residue to floor-level (`0.99×–1.02×` floor, χ² `222.3–274.5`) — indistinguishable from an independent-pair control.
- **Both primitives track the same rates.** BLAKE3 and FNV-1a-per-role agree within ≈ 2 % on the byte-equal floor ratio, within 0.001 on the Hamming bit-diff, and within ≈ 3 % on Scenario A χ² (16 372 vs 15 940 at 4 KB random; 252.7M vs 252.5M at 16 KB near-identical) and inside the same df=255 uniform band under Scenarios B and C, confirming that the barrier's per-scenario behaviour is architectural — the residue floor and its scaling with plaintext-to-container ratio do not depend on the primitive.
- **COBS-alignment probe — the 1-bit-flip near-identical shape does not shift container geometry across the user-facing Barrier Fill range.** Container size depends on per-region COBS-encoded lengths (`containerSizeAuth3_128Cfg(..., cobsLens)`, `itb128.go` / `auth128.go`), and a 1-bit flip that transitions the flipped plaintext byte to / from `0x00` shifts the COBS overhead by one byte; the alignment probe (`TestRedTeamNonceReuseDualNonceCOBSAlignmentProbe`, 200 pairs per cell under Scenario A) sweeps Barrier Fill ∈ {1, 4, 8, 16, 32} × plaintext ∈ {512 B, 4 KB, 16 KB} = 15 cells and measures **0/200 container-body length mismatch at every cell** — even for the two 16 KB pairs whose middle byte flipped to `0x00` (reproducible across every BF row), the container is **quantized by pixel** (`total_pixels × Channels`) and the ±1-byte COBS overhead perturbation sits below the pixel-boundary quantization step across the full BF range measured. The byte-equal residue statistic is therefore not deflated by misaligned-body comparisons at any user-configurable BF: the 18× → 113× → 121× floor ratios reflect the underlying determinism.

**Cross-message plaintext-recovery closure — architectural, held across all three scenarios.** The demasker `nonce_reuse_demask.py` models a Full KPA attacker on a colliding pair under forced nonce reuse: it recovers the per-pixel `(noisePos, rotation)` map by Layer 1 constraint matching against the known plaintexts, on an encoder layout with no Rank Barrier permutation — a layout the shipped construction does not use. Against the shipped 48-bit interlock, the demasker's Layer 1 constraint match requires known region-payload XOR bits at each pixel; those bits are `PEXT48(P1_chunk XOR P2_chunk, m_i_per_chunk)` shifted across lane slots by **Layer II** (`0x06`) and distributed across physical pixels by **Layer I** (`0x05`). Under Scenario A the masks, slot shifts, and sub-region trajectories repeat across the colliding pair, yet under the shipped **Desynchronized Interlocked Barrier** even the mask-oracle lab upper bound (regime B′ of `TestRedTeamNonceReuseCrossMessageDecrypt` and `TestRedTeamNonceReuseLayerBMaskOraclePeek`, granting `[lab-peek: sp_i]` + `[lab-peek: masks]` from the true `lockSeed` — not an attack result) anchors **0 / 63** pixels (`0 / 21` per region) and recovers **0 / 324** content bytes (`0 / 391` total) of a third message, because granting the `0x02` initial entry offset `sp_i = Π_r(0)` via `[lab-peek: sp_i]` and the `lockSeed` masks (`0x03` / `0x06`) still leaves the **Layer I** (`0x05`) `K_r`-sub-region schedule `Π_r` unknown (whereas with `ITB_BARRIERS_DESYNC=off` the same regime B′ oracle recovers 304 / 324 ≈ 94 % of the content bytes, or 315 / 391 ≈ 0.81 over the whole deterministic prefix). The attacker-realistic regimes A and B recover 0 bytes in both postures. Under Scenarios B and C the per-chunk mask triples and slot shifts (or Pixel Barrier schedules) differ across the pair, so even a full-schedule oracle loses coherent alignment; the plaintext-recovery closure is **strictly weaker to the attacker** than Scenario A. In every scenario, no attacker-realistic path anchors a per-pixel `(noisePos, rotation, channelXOR)` triple against the observed body-XOR bytes.

**SAT-based `lockSeed` recovery is structurally unmeasurable at attacker-realism.** Unlike the fresh-nonce regime, the nonce-reuse constraint is formulable under Scenario A: `C1 ⊕ C2` cancels the per-pixel additive mask and hands a Full KPA attacker the known `M1 ⊕ M2`, so each per-chunk observation is a genuine constraint on the interlock permutation with a true satisfying model. Recovery is nonetheless dominated by the `rankToMaskTriple48` combinadic unrank keyed by the `lockSeed` chain, not by the FNV multiply chain a structure-aware solver accelerates: a single 48-bit chunk observation admits ≈ 2^57.80 mask preimages, and collapsing that ambiguity requires coupling many chunks through the shared `lockSeed` chain against the SAT-hostile unrank arithmetic. Under Scenarios B and C the constraint is no longer formulable in that form — each pair carries a different mask triple and slot shift, so the shared-mask cancellation does not apply.

The reuse SAT instance is formulable only when the Pixel Barrier's per-pixel derivations are granted (seven of eight seeds via lab peek — `noiseSeed`, three `dataSeed_i`, three `startSeed_i` including their full `0x05` **Layer I** sub-region schedules), because otherwise the `lockSeed` → per-chunk mask path runs through the **Two-Stage Cascade Fill** — setup derivation `K = deriveInterLockSeed(N_il)` via ChainHash over `[0x04 ‖ N_il]`, then the per-chunk cascade fill over `[0x03 ‖ LE64(groupIdx) ‖ v]` (`v` the 4-byte interlock-nonce fold of `N_il`, one value per message) under `lockComps = [K, Components...]` with Round 1 primer round on `K` and feed-forward through session components (`interlock48_cascade.go`), plus under the **Desynchronized Interlocked Barrier** the **Layer II** cross-lane slot shifts Σ_ℓ over `[0x06 ‖ LE64(i) ‖ v]` (`laneSlotShift` with `desyncBeta0` / `desyncBeta1`, folded via `fullWidthFold` so that `β_0 ≠ β_1 ⇒ μ_0(j) ≠ μ_1(j)` across `M^3` chunk-slot triples; `desync_beta0_ne_beta1`, `desync_no_fixed_slot_pair` in `lean/ITB/Desync.lean`) — before the combinadic unrank and slot shift place the lane fragments, and the instance reduces to PRF preimage recovery on the cascade dominated by the primitive's SAT-hardness rather than the interlock's. But a granted-7/8 attacker is not the nonce-reuse-realistic attacker, who holds only the ciphertext pair and public main nonce `N_m`. The measurable instance and the reuse-realistic instance are **disjoint by construction**: isolating the Rank Barrier's unrank hardness requires stripping the Pixel Barrier, which no reuse-reachable attacker can do. The SAT avenue is therefore not merely unrun but **structurally unmeasurable at attacker-realism** — the closure is PRF-conditional by construction, consistent with the two-layer binding traced above. Empirically corroborated on the adjacent dataSeed_i target: under that probe's maximum-peek lab regime (5 of 8 chains granted, sp_i disclosed), Bitwuzla does not recover the true FNV-1a dataSeed_i lo lane (see [§ FNV-1a lo-lane SAT — architecturally foreclosed](#fnv-1a-lo-lane-sat--architecturally-foreclosed)). The empirical closure rests on the attacker-realistic distinguisher null (matrix above) and the mask-oracle lab upper bound.

**Verdict — plaintext-recovery null across all three scenarios, sample-bounded; near-identical-pair traffic-analysis residue under Scenario A only.** No attacker-realistic probe recovers a byte of a third message encrypted under any of the three dual-nonce collision classes at the tested sample sizes on either primitive. The near-identical-pair body-histogram is the residual signature of Scenario A and scales with plaintext-to-container ratio (18× the 1/256 floor at 512 B → 121× at 16 KB); an outside observer of two Scenario-A ciphertexts can therefore distinguish "near-identical plaintexts" from "independent plaintexts" but the excess bytes carry no plaintext bit, no key bit, and no `noisePos` / `rotation` / `channelXOR` value — every deterministic body byte is `plaintext_region_bit_i XOR channelXOR_i` at 7 of 8 positions with a fresh random bit at the 8th. Both Scenario B and Scenario C dissolve the residue fully to the 1/256 floor (`0.97×–1.07×`, χ² inside the df=255 uniform band) by redrawing one of the two independent axes per encrypt (with **Layer II** `0x06` slot shifts eliminating the partial-alignment residue in Scenario B), and the barrier's plaintext-recovery closure holds a fortiori because the attacker's constraint on the un-collided axis is fresh-nonce-strength. **Dual-slot nonce reuse remains a usage precondition the barrier does not architecturally remove at the traffic-analysis layer** — the mandatory internal nonces are API discipline, and the outer cipher (when engaged) additionally whitens the wire against the near-identical-pair distinguisher.

### Related-seed and related-nonce differential

Neither is reachable through the shipped API — the 8-seed intake draws independent CSPRNG components and rejects both pointer and byte-level `Components` collisions, and the nonces are internal. Forced in the lab (a seed or nonce and its 1-bit-delta twin, every other input fixed): a dataSeed1 delta produces a ciphertext bit-diff fraction of **0.23805** — structurally scoped to one region's third, then diffused by the Rank Barrier's permutation and the COBS / interleave, landing above the one-region floor (≈ 0.167). A lockSeed delta re-draws every mask triple and **Layer II** slot shift and reaches **0.48813**, approaching full avalanche (0.5). Neither channel leaves a low-weight, position-predictable differential — the **8-seed isolation made observable**: each seed's influence is scoped to its own channel and the barrier diffuses the delta rather than localising it. A related-nonce delta perturbs the derivation slots keyed by whichever of the two per-message nonces is touched — a main-nonce Δ perturbs per-pixel `noisePos`, per-region rotation and `channelXOR`, and per-region `startPixel` (`0x02`) and **Layer I** sub-region trajectory (`0x05`) (7 of the 8 seed-derived outputs); an interlock-nonce Δ perturbs the interlock key through `deriveInterLockSeed` (`0x04`, governing `0x03` and **Layer II** `0x06`) — and diffuses at least as thoroughly (empirical measurement below). Any residual related-seed exposure would be an **upstream key-management** defect (a defective KDF supplying correlated seeds), not a barrier property.

**Only the lockSeed axis moves χ² across the barrier's diffusion boundary.** A 1-bit lockSeed Δ re-derives the interlock's per-chunk 48-bit mask triple and **Layer II** cross-lane slot shifts (`0x06`) entirely — the plaintext-byte-to-region split avalanches, every touched pixel byte carries different data bits in the two ciphertexts, and the byte histogram collapses to the df=255 uniform band (max χ² 357.1 CRC128 / 337.4 FNV-1a vs the ~42.1M dataSeed_i ceiling and ~18.7M startSeed_i ceiling) — a residual small enough to be attributed to nonzero permutation-boundary effects at 512 KiB sample size, not primitive leakage. The barrier's related-seed diffusion property is **confined to the lockSeed axis**: every architectural benefit of the interlock at this measurement surface routes through `lockSeed` alone; the other seven seeds' Δ propagates through their own orthogonal channels (`noisePos`, per-region `channelXOR`, per-region `startPixel` `0x02` and **Layer I** sub-region trajectory `0x05`) at whatever bias profile the primitive itself carries, and the barrier does not re-invoke on such Δ paths. This complements the [Nonce reuse](#nonce-reuse-lab-only) closure — the plaintext-recovery closure lives in `lockSeed` and `startSeed_i` secrecy (mask-oracle lab upper bound), and the differential-diffusion closure lives in `lockSeed` as well. Both re-parametrise on `lockSeed` per session, so a production caller's fresh CSPRNG seeds always route both closures through the barrier.

The lockSeed-vs-other axis contrast holds identically across CRC128 and FNV-1a (structural, not primitive-conditional), across the bit0 / bit_mid Δ patterns, and across random / ASCII plaintext (no crib-amplified axis). The bit_high pattern flips the top bit of the last component — the high word of the final ChainHash round — and neither lab primitive carries a high-word change into the low word (independent CRC64 lanes; the T-function multiply): the per-pixel data path (`dataSeed_i`) consumes `hLo` alone, so bit_high is a null Δ on `dataSeed_i` for both primitives (χ² 41.83M–42.13M, at the no-Δ floor). By contrast, on `startSeed_i` the **Layer I** (`0x05`) derivation folds both `(hLo, hHi)` words through `fullWidthFold(hLo, hHi)` without a cancelling primer round, so flipping bit 127 in `hHi` alters `w_0` and `w_1` and re-randomizes that region's sub-region schedule `Π_r`, dropping `startSeed_i` bit_high to **18.57M–18.73M** (the same 1-of-3-region diffusion level as bit0 and bit_mid). The noise position mixes both words, so on the noiseSeed axis the same flip moves every position and lands below the floor. On the lockSeed axis the interlock key consumes both words — the CRC128 flip diffuses inside the high lane and reaches the masks and slot shifts (χ² 254.1 / 325.5), while under FNV-1a on the `0x04 → 0x03 / 0x06` path the flip stays at bit 127 through `deriveInterLockSeed` and is cancelled when the `[K ‖ components]` cascade XORs the flipped component back in at its last round before `fullWidthFold` (χ² 42.09M / 42.03M, the floor). A null Δ is a top-bit-transparency property of the below-spec lab primitive, not a barrier property — a Δ that leaves every derived quantity unchanged has nothing to diffuse. ### CCA — noise-position oracle

Under MAC + Reveal an attacker who submits altered ciphertexts learns the noise position of every physical pixel by single-bit flips: flipping the noise bit of a channel byte leaves the decoded payload and the MAC intact, while flipping any other bit fails authentication. Pixel boundaries (8 bytes each, right after the header) and region boundaries (`third = W·H / 3`) are public, so the oracle yields three per-region arrays of 3-bit positions. The noise counter is not restarted per region and the position is `noiseFold3(h[0] + rotl64(h[1], 1))` over both words of the noise seed's output, so the three arrays are disjoint stretches of one position sequence.

`redteam_noisepos_cca_test.go` runs two attacks against real `EncryptAuthenticated3x128Cfg` wires (35×35 container, 1024-bit keys, 128-bit nonce, 10 messages per primitive, every pixel resolved to exactly one accepted flip across all 8 channel-0 bits, 8P = 9,800 queries/wire; `redteam_noisepos_cca_queries_test.go` measures the early-stop query budget stopping at the first accepted flip, averaging ~4.375 queries/pixel or ~5,359 queries/wire across N = 100 trials, range ~5,130–5,550), using only the oracle's accept / reject answers, the header and public parameters; true start offsets enter the final report only. **Region alignment** searches for a cyclic shift under which two regions' arrays coincide and, when region 3 is one pixel longer, for the window that pins both absolute start offsets; a control layout whose noise counter restarts at 0 in every region recovers the offset exactly. **Public-part match** assumes the position splits into a seed-only 3-bit constant XOR the position computed with an all-zero seed, as a GF(2)-linear primitive's hash output does, and enumerates the constant and `startPixel_i` per region.

## Broken-primitive stress — CRC128 and FNV-1a

The below-spec lab controls stress the shipped construction with the hardest algebraic weaknesses available: a fully **GF(2)-linear** primitive (CRC128, two keyed CRC64 lanes) and an **invertible non-linear** one (FNV-1a, a bijective multiply over Z/2^128 with a triangular low-lane T-function). Neither is a production hash; neither appears in the shipped registry. They lead this discussion because the methodology seeks the hardest stress cases, not the easiest PRF wins. For the companion 8-bit total-PRF-failure suite (`nullHash`, `quadHash`, `trainHash`) and the full-width `jokeHash` T-function control, see [FAQ.md](FAQ.md) and [Reproduction](#reproduction) below.

### CRC128 and FNV-1a "broken" narrative — scope

Both controls leak in isolation — CRC128 through its GF(2)-linearity, FNV-1a through its invertible carry-up low-lane T-function — and the one-region, barrier-off control encodes in this section confirm that the recovery filters are sensitive to that leakage. The shipped API exposes no barrier-off configuration. **Under the shipped barrier, no algebraic leakage from either control is measurable at the tested sample sizes**, and the linear / SAT instance cannot be formulated at all. This is a scope statement, not a safety claim for FNV-1a: the primitive remains below spec, the closure is an instance-formulation argument under the barrier, and total inversion of the primitive still circumvents the barrier.

### CRC128 Crib KPA and Nonce reuse

The recovery runs as `go test -tags redteam -run TestRedTeamBrokenCRC128CribKPA -count=1 ./` (≈ 0.09 s): a 625-candidate startPixel scan against a four-pixel crib, recovering the observable 56-bit compound key over all noise-position and rotation guesses (the Python form of the same filter is `crib_crc128_kpa.py`).

**Verdict — null (0 / 625 anchoring shifts, 0 surviving compound-K candidates, 0 bytes recovered).** Against a one-region, barrier-off control encode in the same test run, the filter anchors exactly one shift and returns one survivor (the true compound key), confirming that the null is a genuine absorption result rather than a filter defect. Against the shipped `Encrypt3x128Cfg` ciphertext (verified round-trip exact), the filter anchors **zero** shifts and returns **zero** survivors across all 625 candidate startPixels: the Rank Barrier's mask permutation and the **Desynchronized Interlocked Barrier** (**Layer II**'s `0x06`-keyed cross-lane slot shifts Σ_ℓ via `laneSlotShift` together with **Layer I**'s `0x05`-keyed K_r-sub-region schedule Π_r via `subregionAddr` / `effectiveStartPixel`; [SCIENCE.md § 1.2.1](SCIENCE.md#121-desynchronized-interlocked-barrier), [PROOFS.md Proof 4a](PROOFS.md#proof-4a-multi-factor-full-kpa-resistance), [PROOFS.md Proof 11](PROOFS.md#proof-11-48-bit-rank-barrier-mask-space-interlocked-barrier)) dislocate the crib bytes below the attacker's predicted post-split positions before pixel encoding ([Barrier crib-anchor displacement](#barrier-crib-anchor-displacement-both-controls) — CRC128: 0–3 in 180 leading crib bytes at the assumed position across runs, at `3 / 180 = 0.0167` on the deterministic seed, below the ≈ 0.0385 same-symbol coincidence floor of the 26-symbol crib), leaving no fixed bit-position-to-lane anchor for the linear system. Moreover, both `0x05` and `0x06` derivations fold `(hLo, hHi)` through `fullWidthFold(hLo, hHi)` (`w_0 = (hLo + rotl64(hHi, 25) + rotl64(hHi, 53)) mod 2^64, w_1 = hHi ⊕ rotl64(w_0, 19) ⊕ rotl64(w_0, 41)`), which preserves 100% output uniformity for every PRF-grade registry primitive (`fullWidthFold_bijection`, `fullWidthFold_preserves_prf_cardinality` in `lean/ITB/Desync.lean`), couples both `CRC64-ECMA` (`hLo`) and `CRC64-ISO` (`hHi`) lanes across all `16/16` seed words (`fullWidthFold_crc128_dual_lane_sensitivity`), and destroys GF(2)-affine separability of `CRC128` via modular addition carries mod `2^64` (`fullWidthFold_non_affine_gf2`). Under a forced nonce collision (`TestRedTeamBrokenCRC128NonceReuse`, ≈ 0.01 s) the Crib KPA still returns zero survivors from either colliding ciphertext: nonce reuse repeats the masks and slot shifts but does not hand the attacker the permutation, so the crib remains unanchored. This scoped absorption claim is bounded by what the filter's Step-1 anchoring gate detects and does not generalise to unrelated attack families; total inversion of the primitive still circumvents the barrier.

**Noise layer under CRC128 with a MAC + Reveal oracle.** On an unshielded control layout (where each region's noise counter starts at `0` or where the 3-bit fold is purely GF(2)-linear), the MAC + Reveal oracle is a single-message attack aimed at recovering the three unknown `startPixel_i` offsets from the observed physical noise-position arrays `obs_i[j]`. Under the shipped `noiseCounterBase` (`i * third + p`) and `noiseMix(word0, word1) = word0 + rotl64(word1, 1)`, single-message `startPixel_i` recovery via cross-region alignment or public-part linear matching closes completely (`0 / 30` in `TestRedTeamNoisePosCCA`), and the dependency inverts into a circular requirement. Under a GF(2)-linear primitive each noise word splits into a seed-only constant XOR a public term, so the position is `noiseFold3((K_lo ⊕ B_lo) + rotl64(K_hi ⊕ B_hi, 1))` with 128 unknown seed bits `(K_lo, K_hi)` and a zero-seed term `B = (B_lo, B_hi)` evaluated at counter `i * third + p`, where under the **Desynchronized Interlocked Barrier** `p` is governed by the secret K_r-sub-region trajectory Π_r (`subregionAddr` / `effectiveStartPixel`, `K_r = 6 + (w_0 mod 5) ∈ [6, 10]` for **Mode 2** `P_r < 365` and `K_r = 8 + (w_0 mod 9) ∈ [8, 16]` for **Mode 1** `P_r ≥ 365`) derived under tag `0x05` via `fullWidthFold`, with a per-message regional trajectory space of `(2^7 · 8! · 18^8)^3 ≈ 2^166.98` at the **Mode 2** floor (`P_r = 147, K_r = 8`, `⌊147/8⌋ = 18` px; `> 2^145` under a lab grant of Π_r(0), and `> 2^104` at `K_r = 6`) and `(4^7 · 8! · 51^8)^3 ≈ 2^224.04` at the **Mode 1** floor (`P_0 = P_1 = 408, P_2 = 409, K_r = 8`, `⌊408/8⌋ = 51` px; `> 2^198` under a lab grant of Π_r(0), and up to `> 2^445` at `K_r = 16`; `subregion_trajectory_bounds_mode2_floor` and `subregion_trajectory_bounds_mode1_floor` in `lean/ITB/Desync.lean`). Because `B` depends on each message's secret regional trajectory Π_r, the attacker cannot even evaluate `B` on a message without already knowing that message's regional schedule. A generic SMT solver (bitwuzla, z3) does not recover the 128 bits from 24 to 4900 revealed positions (one to four messages) within 300–1800 s even with every regional schedule granted; and the specialised linear-cryptanalysis route through the modular-addition carries — estimated at ≈ 2^32–2^34 revealed positions (≈ 2^22–2^24 messages under one seed set, ≈ 2^35–2^37 oracle queries, unmeasured and not a security bound) — likewise requires an out-of-band lab grant of all three per-message regional schedules on every one of those ≈ 2^22–2^24 messages just to form the public inputs `B`. Since a recovered `(K_lo, K_hi)` would yield noise positions only (leaving `dataSeed_i`, DRBG fill, and the Rank Barrier intact), requiring regional schedule grants across millions of messages to recover a constant whose primary utility is finding those offsets is operationally vacuous, and CRC128 is a lab helper outside the shipped registry.

No per-chain observation channel exists. **Instance-formulation closure, not solver-throughput closure.** The SAT harness `sat_harness_4round.py` anchors on the one-region, barrier-off mapping "plaintext byte K → container pixel (startPixel + K/7) mod totalPixels, channel K%7". That SAT instance thus **cannot be written down against the shipped construction** — no chain-inversion tooling converts an unformulable instance into a solvable one. The T-function / Triangle solver at `scripts/redteam/itb/theory/fnv1a/` (100–1000× faster than Bitwuzla on the isolated FNV-1a lo-lane chain by exploiting its `×0x13B` carry-up-only triangular structure) faces the identical observation-layer wall as Bitwuzla against the barrier: the closure is bounded by what the attacker can observe, not by how fast a chain inverter runs.

**Positive control corroborated.** `TestRedTeamBrokenFNV1aCribKPAControl` drives the same 8-seed FNV-1a configuration through the low-level `process128Cfg` encoder (a one-region, barrier-off control encode — not reachable through the shipped API) and confirms the anchor logic recovers the true xor_mask56 at every one of the first 6 crib pixels under true (sp, np, r), matching the naive-crib SAT anchoring premise bit-exact. The barrier null is contrasted against a filter that **is** sensitive on the barrier-off control encode.

No delta position dominates the ranking: `end_bit0` produces the maximum at 512 B (`308.55`) but drops to 244.63 at 16 KB, while `mid_bit3` produces the highest cell at 16 KB (296.07) and 4 KB (274.24) but sits at 239.21 at 128 B. Size sensitivity is flat: per-size mean chi² is **247 / 268 / 255 / 249** across the four sizes, no monotone trend as plaintext size scales two orders of magnitude. Under fresh nonces each `Encrypt3x128Cfg` call redraws all eight nonce-bound derivation slots across the Rank and Pixel Barriers (as in the single-message CPA probe above and [Nonce reuse](#nonce-reuse-lab-only)), so the two pair wires share no per-position determinism and the near-identical delta on the plaintext cannot survive the independent Rank Barrier mask redraws.

**Nonce reuse contrast.** On the same near-identical-pair shape under **forced nonce reuse** (see [Nonce reuse](#nonce-reuse-lab-only), Scenario A), the byte-equal rate reaches ≈ **0.070** ( ≈ **18.0 ×** the 1/256 floor) at 512 B — the Scenario A traffic-analysis residue. Under fresh nonces the same shape sits at the 1/256 floor across every tested (size, delta) cell, at a matrix-maximum near-identical floor ratio of **1.027 ×** at size = 512 B, delta = `spread_hw4`. The independent-pair control's floor ratio maxes at **1.035 ×** on a different cell (size = 4 KB, delta = `spread_hw4`), higher than the near-identical maximum — the two categories are indistinguishable at this resolution because the finite-sample sampling noise across cells is larger than any near-identical-vs-independent contrast the barrier permits under fresh nonces. **The near-identical-pair distinguisher is a nonce-reuse-only artefact.** The mandatory internal nonce alone is the load-bearing discipline against the cross-message traffic-analysis distinguisher — a **≈ 17.5 ×** collapse of the near-identical floor ratio between the two regimes on the 512 B reference cell (for outer-cipher whitening of the cleartext dimension header, see [§4.0](#40-layer-attribution--what-actually-appears-on-the-wire)).

**Cross-attack synthesis.** The passive Full KPA null (F1 / F2 / F4), the single-message CPA null (FNV-1a↔BLAKE3 homogeneity in-band on every chosen-plaintext kind), and the cross-message pair-shape null (this probe) together cover the passive → active × single-message → cross-message attacker-capability spectrum against the below-spec primitive under fresh nonces. Chosen-plaintext-pair freedom does not restore an observation channel any more than chosen-plaintext-message freedom did, because each `Encrypt3x128Cfg` call redraws every derived per-message secret from the fresh nonce and neither the single-message wire byte distribution nor the pair-shape byte-XOR distribution surfaces a primitive-attributable handle.

### Barrier crib-anchor displacement (both controls)

A structural probe (`TestRedTeamBrokenBarrierDisplacement`, ≈ 0.07 s; holds true seeds to observe **what the barrier did**, not an attack path) encrypts a fully attacker-known structured crib and measures the fraction of leading crib bytes still sitting at their assumed post-split position. Anchor destruction is **primitive-agnostic** — it does not depend on any algebraic property the primitive lacks, which is why the same mechanism closes the CRC128 linear collapse and the FNV-1a lo-lane SAT.

## PRF-grade closure vs. broken-primitive under-determination

Under the barrier both primitive classes converge to the same observable outcome — no measurable signal at the tested sample sizes — by two different mechanisms.

**PRF-grade branch — definitional closure, one representative sufficient.** For a PRF-grade primitive the null is a **definitional consequence of the PRF assumption**, not an accident of the tested sample: a secure PRF's ChainHash output is computationally indistinguishable from random before the barrier is ever applied, so the attack surface reduces to statistical ciphertext properties already at the finite-sample floor. The barrier adds instance-formulation under-determination on top of an already-null primitive; the PRF assumption alone would suffice, and the barrier is redundant-but-additive on this branch. Each registry member enters the construction through the same ChainHash interface and is consumed by the same barrier; expanding empirical breadth across every member would multiply runs without changing the argument. The representative-primitive approach documents the null rigorously on BLAKE3 and generalises through the shared assumption. Probe 1 and Probe 4 corroborate the null empirically. The positive controls in this document drive below-spec primitives through a one-region, barrier-off control encode.

**Below-spec branch — architectural under-determination, primitive-independent.** The below-spec primitive leaks in isolation, but under the barrier the attacker cannot formulate the linear or invertible system at all: the bit-position-to-lane mapping is a hidden per-chunk ≈ 2^70.20 secret compounded by **Layer II** cross-lane slot shifts (`0x06`) and **Layer I** sub-region schedules (`0x05`), so the GF(2)-linear pixel-independent collapse that recovers the CRC128 key on a one-region, barrier-off control encode has no known analogue against the shipped construction. The closure is an instance-formulation argument that does not invoke the PRF assumption for the primitive itself — it rests on the barrier's own reduction arithmetic and its per-chunk PRF-keyed mask draw, and holds under fresh nonces conditional on the absence of a structural shortcut against that arithmetic. It is not an independently certified result.

**Equivalence of outcome.** The barrier's mask-formulation under-determination is the dominant term across the tested primitive spectrum. For a PRF the primitive supplies the null and the barrier is redundant-but-additive; for a broken primitive the barrier supplies the null the primitive fails to.

## Phase 4 — Construction-level creative probes (Triple + Interlocked Barrier)

This phase targets the **construction itself** — properties that do not depend on which hash keys the barrier. Each probe drives either the Triple facade wire or the barrier kernels directly and reports a sample-bounded verdict; every wire statistic is computed from bytes an attacker holding the ciphertext already has. The probes ship as Go tests (`harness_test.go` for kernel probes, `triple/harness_wire_test.go` for wire probes), gating their full-sample loops behind `ITB_HARNESS_FULL=1`.

### 4.0 Layer attribution — what actually appears on the wire

The barrier-only wire (`[prefix(32)][main_nonce][W][H][container]`, wrapper off) is uniform at the byte-value level **except for the cleartext container-dimension header**. The container body is individually uniform: every payload byte — COBS data, the COBS terminator `0x00`, and the DRBG tail fill alike — passes through the Rank Barrier's lane permutation, COBS framing, and the Pixel Barrier (channelXOR + rotate7 + noise-insert) before reaching the wire, so no payload byte survives as its pre-barrier value. Measured at N = 3000–4000 256-byte messages, the container-body zero rate sits at the 1/256 floor (≈ 0.391 %), and no payload-derived byte value is over-represented above the sampling floor.

The one structural signature is the **big-endian `W` and `H` dimension fields** written in cleartext ahead of the container (`out = prefix ‖ main_nonce ‖ W ‖ H ‖ container` in `Encrypt3x256Cfg`). For a fixed plaintext size the container is a deterministic size, so these four bytes are identical across messages: their high bytes are `0x00` and their low bytes are the dimension value. Pooled over many messages this over-represents the `0x00` bin (the two high bytes) and one companion value (the shared low byte) by a **relative ≈ +5 %** each — an **absolute** excess of roughly 0.02 percentage points on the `0x00` rate. A byte-histogram chi-square, the sharp instrument for granular bias, detects it (barrier-only per-profile χ² ≈ 1718–2028 at N = 8000, df = 255, uniform band [142, 368]); Shannon entropy stays **≈ 7.99998 bits/byte** and the wire is incompressible (flate ratio 1.0002).

Two consequences fix the scope of this signature:

- **It is not a confidentiality leak.** The dimension header restates the container size, which the wire length already reveals. The COBS terminator is **not** on the wire — it is absorbed by the Pixel Barrier, so its position (set by the secret per-region `startPixel`, the **Layer I** sub-region trajectory `Π_r`, and the payload length) is nowhere observable. The signature carries no plaintext, no key, no terminator position, and no startPixel or sub-region trajectory. Confidentiality against COA / KPA / CPA is delivered by the barrier composition itself, not by any wire-whitening pass.
- **The outer cipher removes it for format deniability.** Engaging the wrapper encrypts the whole wire including the dimension header, dropping the byte chi-square into the uniform band (≈ 231–249) and the `0x00` deviation from +5 % to ± 0.1 %. **The outer cipher delivers wire-level format deniability — a traffic-analysis / format-fingerprint property — not confidentiality**, which is the barrier's job. This scopes every "indistinguishable from random" byte-histogram claim: at the barrier-only layer the claim holds for the container body and the payload-channel probes; the cleartext dimension header is the sole exception and is whitened by the wrapper.

The finding is primitive-agnostic: the dimension header is written identically regardless of which hash keys the seeds, so a broken primitive and a PRF-grade primitive produce the same signature when the wrapper is off. It is not a primitive leak.

### 4.1 Probe C1 — cross-profile wire distinguishability and mode ambiguity

**Question.** Can a wire observer tell the **Streaming AEAD** MAC profile from the **Non-AEAD** profile from byte statistics alone? The mode-ambiguity doctrine ships both with a wire-identical envelope so a MITM cannot assume a MAC-check oracle exists.

**Setup.** The facade encrypts N = 8000 fresh-random 256-byte messages under each profile, parallax off, at both wrapper postures. The load-bearing statistic is the two-sample **homogeneity** chi-square between the AEAD and Non-AEAD pools (df = 255).

| Posture | AEAD wire χ² | Non-AEAD wire χ² | 0x00 dev (both) | AEAD↔Non-AEAD homogeneity χ² |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wrapper off (barrier only) | 2028.0 | 1717.6 | +4.9–5.4 % | **251.7** |
| Wrapper on (outer cipher) | 249.2 | 231.4 | ±0.1 % | **251.5** |

**Verdict — mode ambiguity holds (sample-bounded).** The homogeneity chi-square is **inside the uniform band whether or not the wrapper is engaged** (251.7 / 251.5, band top 368). The two modes carry one identical container law — the dimension-header signature is identical in both, so it cancels in the two-sample test — and no wire byte statistic separates them at the tested sample size. The MAC rides inside the encryption envelope; the Non-AEAD path fills the corresponding region with DRBG dummy of matching length. **Positive control:** the barrier-only per-profile chi-square (2028 / 1718, far above the band) confirms the probe detects the signature that is actually present, so the in-band homogeneity result is a real null. Empty input is rejected at the `triple.Pipeline` layer with `ErrEmptyInput`, so no zero-payload wire exists to distinguish across profiles; mode ambiguity is trivially preserved across all valid input sizes.

### 4.2 Probe C2 — mask-space structural uniformity and the gcd anti-collapse trap

**Question.** Does the per-chunk mask-triple derivation realise the architectural ≈ 2^70.20 balanced-partition space, or does an implementation detail collapse it? This is a white-box structural harness, not an attack.

**Result.** Over N = 200000 random draws (gcd witness at N = 500000): every triple satisfies `popcount = 16` per lane, `m0 | m1 | m2 = 0x0000_FFFF_FFFF_FFFF`, and pairwise-disjoint lanes (100 % of draws); per-bit balance within 2.28 σ across all 48 positions; reduced-index chi-square 261.7 / 265.5 (band [142, 368]). The gcd anti-collapse trap: under the shipped two-step reduction the fraction landing on `idx0 ≡ idx1 (mod 66861)` is 8.00 × 10⁻⁶ (4 draws), consistent with the full-space expectation 1/66861 ≈ 1.496 × 10⁻⁵ (≈ 7.5 draws); the rejected same-rank double-mod would confine **every** draw to that diagonal.

**Verdict — null structural deviation.** The mask space is full and balanced and the two-step reduction avoids the residue-class trap, corroborating the Rank Barrier cardinality claim at the derivation layer. This is a structural-correctness result under the standing assumption that the lockSeed's PRF output is indistinguishable from uniform, not a security proof. Primitive-agnostic; the per-attack tracks cite C2 rather than re-running it.

### 4.3 Probe C3 — tail-fill DRBG residue, size-stability

The Non-AEAD profile (wrapper off) encrypts N = 8000 wires at plaintext sizes {1, 6, 32, 4096} bytes. Entropy stays 7.99998 and flate 1.0002 at every size; the dimension-header `0x00` relative deviation ranges only over [4.91 %, 5.26 %] across the four-order size span. **Verdict — null size-leak:** the tail-fill-dominated small-plaintext wire carries the same container law as the payload-dominated large-plaintext wire, so no size-dependent fill anomaly is present at the tested sample size beyond the length the wire already reveals.

### 4.4 Probe C4 — cross-region independence

The kernel probe (C4b) drives `chunk48lock` with N = 200000 random chunks under fresh mask triples; the wire probe (C4a) aligns N = 30000 wires column-wise. **C4b:** max |r| = 0.00180 against the sampling floor 0.00224 — cross-lane correlation at the floor. **C4a:** max |r| = 0.02192 against floor 0.00577 — ~3.8× the floor, below the 8× threshold; the attribution is the marginal dimension-header / container-framing residual, **not** a barrier cross-region leak (the kernel probe, which bypasses framing, shows none). **Verdict — null cross-region coupling at the barrier; a small container-framing residual at the wire** the outer cipher would remove.

### 4.5 Probe C5 — cumulative bias floor

The kernel probe pools lane-output bits over N = 300000 chunks; the wire probe pools N = 4000 wires of 4096-byte messages at both postures. **C5-core:** max per-bit deviation 1.903 × 10⁻³ against floor 1.826 × 10⁻³ (maxZ 2.09) — the architectural per-chunk bias 2^-57.8 is ~15 orders of magnitude below detectability. **C5 wire:** barrier-only 2.987 × 10⁻⁴ (container tilt), wrapped 1.188 × 10⁻⁴ against floor 1.588 × 10⁻⁴. **Verdict — no measurable cumulative bias at the tested sample size** (turning the granularity into a distinguisher needs ≈ 2^115.6 chunks); the small barrier-only tilt is the dimension-header signature the outer cipher removes.

### 4.6 Probe C6 — nonce-freshness and per-message divergence

A single session encrypts the same 256-byte plaintext N = 10000 times (fresh internal nonce per call). **Result:** 10000 distinct wires out of 10000, mean pairwise Hamming distance 0.4998 of the body bits (ideal 0.5). **Verdict — null collision:** the internal per-call nonce drives fresh masks, noise positions, and per-region offsets, so the wire body is effectively an independent random draw per message — empirical corroboration of the fresh-nonce under-determination without enumerating the ≈ 2^57.80 preimage space.

## Cross-track synthesis

The tracks converge on one sample-bounded, PRF-conditional outcome — **no measurable signal at the tested sample sizes under the stated threat models** — reached through complementary evidence:

- **Complementary primitive tracks ([PRF-grade closure vs. broken-primitive under-determination](#prf-grade-closure-vs-broken-primitive-under-determination)).** The broken-primitive empty compound-key survivor sets and the PRF-grade floor-level crib / pairwise byte-equal rates corroborate the same architectural boundary from opposite ends of the primitive spectrum.
- **Shared barrier core.** Both tracks rest on the Rank Barrier mask-space cardinality (≈ 2^70.20 in 48-bit mode, or ≈ 2^258.60 in 168-bit mode), **Layer I/II** desynchronized schedules (`0x05` / `0x06`), and 3-region independence, corroborated at the derivation and kernel layers by [Probe C2](#42-probe-c2--mask-space-structural-uniformity-and-the-gcd-anti-collapse-trap) and [Probe C4](#44-probe-c4--cross-region-independence).
- **Wire-level scoping ([§4.0](#40-layer-attribution--what-actually-appears-on-the-wire)).** The barrier absorbs every payload byte including the COBS terminator; byte-histogram uniformity claims are scoped to the container body and payload-channel probes, while the outer cipher whitens the cleartext dimension header for format deniability.

## Conclusion

The validation pass produces a uniform null verdict at the tested sample sizes across the broken-primitive stress controls, the PRF-grade representative, and the construction-level probes. The one condition that surfaces any signal is lab-forced dual-slot nonce reuse, and even there the near-identical-pair residue yields no plaintext-recovery channel.

The architectural closure rests on the **Desynchronized Interlocked Barrier**: **Rank Barrier** (the 48-bit or 168-bit interlock permutation paired with **Layer II** cross-lane slot shifts under `0x06`, multiplying single-chunk slot matching by `M^3` to `|Ω_chunk| · M^3 ≈ 2^96.91`–`2^101.53` in `"48"` and `≈ 2^279.89`–`2^284.51` in `"168"` at the 1024-bit **Mode 2** / **Mode 1** floors, or `≈ 2^128.91`–`2^133.53` and `≈ 2^311.89`–`2^316.51` with the 4-byte interlock-nonce fold `v`) moves the KPA / CPA closure to the instance-formulation layer — a known crib anchors no fixed bit-to-lane mapping, and each mask triple has ≈ 2^57.80 preimages per chunk among ≈ 2^70.20 masks in 48-bit mode (or ≈ 2^253.40 preimages among ≈ 2^258.60 masks in 168-bit mode) with no ranking signal — while **Pixel Barrier** (per-pixel channelXOR / rotate7 / noise across the **Layer I** sub-region schedule under `0x05`, spanning `≈ 2^166.98` / `≈ 2^224.04` 3-region trajectories at `K_r = 8` on the 1024-bit **Mode 2** / **Mode 1** floors) denies a per-byte observation channel at the 1:1 signal/noise floor ([§4.0](#40-layer-attribution--what-actually-appears-on-the-wire)).

All verdicts are sample-bounded and, where they invoke primitive strength, PRF-conditional. The closure is an architectural claim corroborated by these measurements, not an independently certified result. Total or systematic PRF inversion circumvents the barrier; nonce reuse leaves a near-identical-plaintext traffic-analysis distinguisher the barrier does not remove but no attacker-realistic plaintext-recovery channel at the tested sample size; and upstream key-management, side channels, key compromise, implementation defects, and CCA via a deployment decryption oracle sit outside what the barrier itself closes and are treated at the construction level in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [PROOFS.md](PROOFS.md).
